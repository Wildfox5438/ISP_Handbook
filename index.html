Miranda Rights/Warning

Part 1: You have the right to remain silent.

Part 2: Anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law.

Part 3: You have the right to talk to a lawyer and have him present with you while you are being questioned.

Part 4: If you cannot afford to hire a lawyer, one will be appointed to represent you before any questioning if you wish.

Part 5: You can decide at any time to exercise these rights and not answer any questions or make any statements.

Part 6: Do you understand each of these rights I have explained to you? Having these rights in mind, do you wish to talk to us now?

Weapon & Uniform Regulations

All on-duty officers must use approved firearms only. Use of non-approved firearms requires Major+ approval.

  • Primary: Glock 17 (Required)
  • Secondary (Trunk Stored): M4A1, Fabarm FP6 (Sergeant+)
  • Approved Division Weapons: Glock 17, SIG P226, M4A1, MP5, SPAS 12 (Sergeant+), Fabarm FP6 (Sergeant+)

Uniform Regulations

  • Uniforms: ISP Class A (Under working), Class B, Class C, Formal (Major+ only)
  • Allowed Hats: Police Baseball Caps (Black only), Police Beanies (Black only), No Hat
  • Allowed Eyewear: Shades, Eye Glasses (No rainbow lenses)
  • Duty Belts: Use lockers
  • Plate Carriers/Body Armor: Black or Dark Blue
  • Bodycams: Allowed with command approval

Vehicle Regulations
