Miranda Rights/Warning

Part 1: You have the right to remain silent.

Part 2: Anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law.

Part 3: You have the right to talk to a lawyer and have him present with you while you are being questioned.

Part 4: If you cannot afford to hire a lawyer, one will be appointed to represent you before any questioning if you wish.

Part 5: You can decide at any time to exercise these rights and not answer any questions or make any statements.

Part 6: Do you understand each of these rights I have explained to you? Having these rights in mind, do you wish to talk to us now?
